# 坦克动荡：游戏规则与 Bot 速查（v7）

1. 两到八辆坦克在随机迷宫对战，普通弹可反弹、可打死自己；一发命中即击毁。每辆坦克最多同时有十枚普通弹，7.5 个游戏秒后消失。普通弹速度默认每 tick 5.2 像素，可以在开局配置；特殊武器不变。
2. 只剩一辆坦克时还要撑过 3 秒，期间场上弹丸和地雷仍会伤人。活到最后才加一分；同归于尽不加分。每小局更换迷宫，默认先拿三分者获胜，也可改为打满指定局数。
3. 每游戏秒 60 tick。每 tick 一条动作：`throttle` 和 `turn` 都取 -1/0/1，`fire` 为布尔值。角度以弧度计，0 向右，顺时针为正。按住开火与重新按下不是一回事，使用地雷和手动引爆时尤其重要。
4. 碰到道具箱就会装备；手上已有武器时不能再拿，接触时会提示。默认地图是 12×9 格。具体武器的充能、发射、引爆和命中顺序见下方。
5. `tick` 是整场计数，`roundTick` 每小局重置；弹丸、地雷、道具的实体 ID 只在各小局内唯一。复盘请用小局号和 ID 一起追踪。

6. 每小局默认第 40 秒开始收缩矩形安全区，持续 15 秒，两项都可调整。留在区外满 2 秒会被淘汰，回到区内会重置计时。最后一辆存活的坦克在 3 秒结算期内无敌。

## Reading the observation and issuing actions

- The simulated clock runs at **60 ticks per game second**. All bots act on the same tick N; accepted actions determine state N+1. Game time is independent of UI and hardware speed.
- Submit exactly one action payload: `{"throttle":1,"turn":-1,"fire":false}`. Throttle −1/0/+1 reverses, stops, or advances. Turn −1/0/+1 turns counterclockwise, stays, or turns clockwise. `fire` is the pressed state for **this tick**. A new press after release matters for Laser, frag and mines. A neutral action is all zero/false. No mouse coordinates or keycodes are accepted over the bot protocol.
- Observation includes the receiving `playerId`, current tick, arena width/height, full `walls`, all `tanks`, `bullets`, `pickups`, `mines`, remaining settlement deadline where present, and result where present. `shellLimit` gives the live ordinary-shell cap (10). `shellSpeed` gives the configured initial speed of ordinary shells (px/tick). `pickupBlocks` lists current blocked crate contacts (`tankId`, `pickupId`). Coordinates are in pixels, right = +x, down = +y. Angle 0 points right; angle increases clockwise in radians.
- Tank fields include `alive`, `cooldown`, `weapon`, `weaponAmmo`, `weaponCharge`, `weaponGap`, `fireHeld`, `fragId`, and `aimAngle`. Projectile fields include `owner`, `kind`, `sourceId`, position, velocity (px/tick), `ttl` (advances remaining), and whether it can hit its owner. Track projectiles, mines and crates by their IDs within the round. For fairness/debugging the full-state bot interface reveals even dormant mines; this differs from human visibility. Internal RNG state/counters are not in bot observations.

## Round and movement

- Two tanks start in seeded opposite corners; three start in distinct seeded corners, as before. For four through eight tanks, choose distinct seeded grid-cell centers, maximizing the distance to the closest previously chosen center at each selection; each tank faces a seeded open exit. This allows even small arenas without overlapping bodies. Each round generates a connected maze with seeded loop openings. The maze complexity setting changes the chance of removing a remaining interior wall: dense 0%, classic 22%, open 60%. The initial maze is connected at every setting. Default size is 12×9 cells (768×576 px); custom dimensions remain 4–16 columns and 4–12 rows. Cells are 64 px square, walls are axis-aligned line segments. No wall destruction.
- Movement order: rotate 0.06 rad/tick, then move up to 2.2 px/tick in that direction. Tank collision radius is 12 px. Wall penetration cancels travel but not rotation. Overlapping candidate tank positions cancel both moves, repeating conflict checks after rewinds. In four- through eight-seat matches, each conflict pass checks the same proposed positions before rewinding any conflicts, rather than giving one seat priority. A tank cannot kill by pushing in this reconstruction.
- A surviving tank may keep acting for 180 ticks (3 simulation seconds) after becoming the sole survivor. Existing projectiles and mines stay live, but become harmless to the sole survivor. If every tank is destroyed by effects of the same tick, no one scores; otherwise the lone tank wins at its settlement tick. More than one survivor at `maxTicks` draws. No new crates spawn during settlement, but the surviving tank may collect crates already on the map until the round ends. No respawn inside a round.

## Shrinking safe zone (v6)

- `shrinkAfterSeconds` is a whole number from 0 to 120, default 40; `shrinkDurationSeconds` is a whole number from 1 to 120, default 15. Each round restarts the clock. The rectangular safe zone always keeps the arena's aspect ratio and initially covers the board. Its edges move inward linearly during the configured duration, then hold in place (shorter final half-span: 24 px). Its fixed center is the center of the nearest cell to the geometric arena midpoint, choosing the upper/left cell if there is a tie.
- Observation/state `zone` is `{x,y,halfWidth,halfHeight,initialHalfWidth,initialHalfHeight,finalHalfWidth,finalHalfHeight,startTick,endTick,graceTicks,outsideTicks}`. Start and end are **round-relative** ticks. `outsideTicks` maps seat IDs to consecutive outside ticks. Forecast any half-extent at round tick `t` by interpolating from its initial to final extent using `clamp((t-startTick)/(endTick-startTick),0,1)`.
- A tank whose center stays strictly outside any edge for 120 consecutive ticks (2 game seconds) is eliminated. Re-entry, including exact edge contact, resets the counter. Shots and mines resolve before the zone; zone eliminations are simultaneous and emit `zone-eliminated` with `tankId,x,y`, not a kill credited to a player. Walls, crates, and shells are not altered by the zone.
- After all damage and zone eliminations are resolved for a tick, if exactly one tank remains, its observed `invulnerable` flag becomes true. During the existing 180-tick settlement, shots and beams pass through it, it cannot trigger mines, and zone exposure is reset and ignored. If nobody survives the initial tick, there is still no winner.
- Unless set explicitly, the round timeout is the greater of 60 seconds and the configured start plus duration plus 5 seconds. The separate host match tick limit can still end a series.

## Shells and weapons

- One hit destroys a tank. Ordinary shells travel 5.2 px/tick by default (312 px per game second; `shellSpeed` accepts finite decimal values from 3 to 9), have 3 px collision radius, and ricochet off walls and wall endpoints. Exactly simultaneous corner contact reverses components; a wall contact ties ahead of a tank hit. The owner is protected only until the shell exits the initial hull radius or bounces. Shells do not collide with one another. Max **ten live ordinary shells per tank**; slot opens on hit/expiration. Normal cooldown is 24 ticks and each shell expires after 450 ticks (7.5s), including its launch advance.
- Crates spawn at unoccupied cell centers at least 30 px from living tanks, every 360 ticks by default, up to four present. Collect by bringing the tank’s 12 px body radius within 2 px of the visible 16×16 px crate square, including diagonal corners. You cannot replace a held weapon; a first blocked contact emits `pickup-blocked` with `reason: "weapon-slot-occupied"` and the `heldWeapon`, with no repeats until the pair separates. An equipped primary weapon remains until consumed. Closest eligible tank wins a race; exact ties are seeded, not process-order races. Pickups resolve after firing, so a newly equipped crate can be used from the next tick. A `pickup` event includes `activation` (`hold` or `press`), `weaponAmmo`, and `releaseRequired` when a fresh-press weapon was collected with fire already held: send one `fire:false` tick, then press. The crate type is chosen from the following five weapons:
  - **Laser / 瞄准激光** (`laser`): a dotted, walls-only `aimGuide` previews up to 256 px. A fresh press consumes the pickup and fires an instantaneous lethal ray, reflecting off walls and stopping at the first tank, 576 px or 16 segments, whichever ends the path first. It may hit its owner after a bounce. It reserves no shell slot. The preview ignores tanks; it is not a guarantee of a hit.
  - **Death Ray** (`death-ray`): hold fire for 90 ticks. Hull and aim lock while holding the charge. Release cancels the charge without consuming the weapon. At full charge a single lethal ray passes through walls and can hit multiple opponents. The tank returns to normal fire after use. This is the through-wall weapon, not the separate classic targeting-laser pickup.
  - **Gatling** (`gatling`): hold fire for 18 charge ticks; emit 18 small shells, one every 5 held/ready ticks at 7.5 px/tick. Releasing fire pauses firing, preserving charge/ammo. The deterministic fan varies aim by up to 0.045 rad. Shell life is 90 ticks. They ricochet, can injure their owner, and do not use normal shell slots.
  - **Mines** (`mine`): up to three placements behind the hull, one per fresh press. Arm 30 ticks after placement. A nearby tank within 18 px triggers the armed mine; the owner can trigger it only after moving more than 20 px away. Blast emits 12 shrapnel shells with stable source IDs.
  - **Frag bomb** (`frag`): one fresh press launches a bouncing bomb at 4.5 px/tick. Another fresh press detonates it; impact or 240-tick expiry also detonates it. The bomb itself can hit. The blast emits 16 shrapnel shells. A dead owner cannot manually detonate, but the existing projectile remains live.
- Shrapnel first moves the following tick; it bounces, travels at 5 px/tick, and lasts 60 advances. It can hit the tank that created it. Special-weapon ammunition does not count against the regular ten-shell cap.
- Within each tick: move tanks, fire weapons, trace projectile movement, trigger mines, create burst fragments, then resolve impacts and round status, then collect/spawn crates when applicable. A fired weapon or projectile survives a same-tick owner death. Hits are gathered against the same pre-damage tank state, so simultaneous deaths are possible. Same-time tank hit ties are resolved in stable seat order. Logs contain shot, bounce, hit, destruction, expiry, crate, and weapon/burst events with stable within-round IDs.

Config bounds are in [config-v6.json](schema/config-v7.json); the [action schema](schema/action-v7.json) describes only the game payload, not its transport envelope. RC missile, homing missile, exact original maze distribution and tuning, Flash UI input behavior, and collision-based kills are not implemented. For deterministic re-simulation, use the same implementation, runtime, config, player order, seed, and accepted action stream.

## Match wrapper and replay

- Default match: the first tank to **three round wins**. A draw awards no point; the score persists across rounds. After an unresolved series round ends, the last frame stays for 60 ticks before the next seeded maze. These scoring and safety limits are this platform's match format, not a claim about when Flash stopped play.
- `tick` is the global match clock; `roundTick` resets at each new maze, `round` starts at 1, and `scores` is keyed by seat. `phase` is `playing` or `intermission` in observations; actions during intermission have no effect. `settleUntil` is relative to `roundTick`. Use both `round` and entity ID to link events across a match.
- The CLI can override the score target with `--score-to-win 1` (range 1–10). There is a host tick limit; hitting it before the target ends with no match winner. This limit is separate from the round `maxTicks`.
- Human controls: A uses arrow keys and M (or Space); B uses ESDF and Q; in three-human play, C moves the mouse and clicks the arena. All of these are translated into the same accepted action payload as a bot.

## 队伍、友伤和固定局数（v7）

- 默认是自由对战。组队时给所有参赛席位分配队伍，至少两队、最多四队；每队可以有一人或多人。界面显示 A/B/C/D，配置和记录中的队号是字符串 `"1"` 到 `"4"`。
- `friendlyFire` 默认关闭：友军不挡同队的炮弹或激光，同队火力不能伤害队友，也不会由队友触发地雷。自己的反弹炮弹和自己触发的地雷仍能伤到自己；缩圈不受友伤开关影响。开启友伤后，同队伤害生效，但不记在 UI 的对敌击杀统计里。
- 当同一 tick 的伤害全部结算后只剩一队，该队全体幸存者在结算期无敌；该队只加一分，不按幸存人数加分。全灭或超时多队仍在场，是小局平局。
- `roundLimit: 0` 或省略保留先到目标分数（默认 3）；设为 1–20 等于打满指定总局数，平局也占一局，结束时比分最高的选手或队伍获胜，最高分并列则整场平局。
- Bot 仍用 JSONL v1。组队局观测有 `teams`（按席位）、`friendlyFire`、`teamScores` 以及 `roundWinnerTeam`；未适配队伍的旧策略能运行，但不会自动识别或配合队友。整场结果中的队伍赢家使用 `winnerTeam`（不是选手 ID）。
